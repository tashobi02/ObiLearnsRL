# Unit 1 Papers

The PDFs in this folder support the [Unit 1 study notes](../Notes/Unit1.md). Each PDF is stored beside this index.

## Suggested reading order

| Order | Paper | Main topic | Source |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Sutton & Barto — *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, 2nd ed.](sutton-barto-RL-2nd-ed.pdf) (2018) | RL foundations and value methods | [Book PDF](http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf) |
| 2 | [Mnih et al. — DQN](mnih-dqn-2013.pdf) (2013) | Deep value learning and experience replay | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5602) |
| 3 | [Williams — REINFORCE](williams-reinforce-1992.pdf) (1992) | Monte Carlo policy gradients | [Paper](http://www-anw.cs.umass.edu/~barto/courses/cs687/Williams92Simple.pdf) |
| 4 | [Mnih et al. — A3C](mnih-a3c-2016.pdf) (2016) | Asynchronous actor-critic learning | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01783) |
| 5 | [Schulman et al. — TRPO](schulman-trpo-2015.pdf) (2015) | Constrained policy updates | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05477) |
| 6 | [Schulman et al. — PPO](schulman-ppo-2017.pdf) (2017) | Clipped policy optimization | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) |
| 7 | [Raffin et al. — Stable-Baselines3](Stable-Baseline3.pdf) (2020 preprint) | Reliable RL implementations | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08948) |

## File note

The local DQN PDF may be an arXiv abstract wrapper rather than the full paper. For the full article, use the [Nature version](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16936) or [arXiv v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.5602v1).
