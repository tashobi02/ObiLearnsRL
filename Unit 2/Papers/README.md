# Unit 2 Papers

The PDFs are locally saved copies of foundational work relevant to the [Unit 2 notes](../Notes/Unit2.md).

| Reading order | Paper | Why it matters | Original source |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Sutton — *Learning to Predict by the Methods of Temporal Differences* (1988)](sutton-temporal-differences-1988.pdf) | Introduces temporal-difference learning and bootstrapping. | [Paper PDF](https://jmvidal.cse.sc.edu/library/sutton88a.pdf) |
| 2 | [Watkins — *Learning from Delayed Rewards* (1989)](watkins-learning-from-delayed-rewards-1989.pdf) | Doctoral thesis introducing Q-learning in the delayed-reward setting. | [Author's thesis PDF](https://www.cs.rhul.ac.uk/~chrisw/new_thesis.pdf) |
| 3 | [Watkins & Dayan — *Q-learning* (1992)](watkins-dayan-q-learning-1992.pdf) | Defines the tabular update and proves convergence under its assumptions. | [Dayan's paper PDF](https://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~dayan/papers/cjch.pdf) |

The [Gymnasium FrozenLake](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/toy_text/frozen_lake/) and [Taxi](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/toy_text/taxi/) pages document the environments used in the notebook.
